# :material-shield-lock: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 March 2026*

## Overview

**Music Chamber Blog** is a personal, non-commercial blog run by Carsten Vuellings,
published at [soundphilosopher.github.io/music-chamber-blog](https://soundphilosopher.github.io/music-chamber-blog/).
It covers music discussions, release lists, and top picks.

This blog does **not** actively collect, store, or process any personal data about
its visitors. There is no user registration, no contact form, no comment system,
and no analytics or tracking of any kind operated by this site.

The sections below explain the technical context in which third-party services may
come into contact with data when you visit.

---

## Hosting: GitHub Pages

This blog is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), a service
operated by **GitHub, Inc.** (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When you
visit any page on this blog, your request is handled by GitHub's servers. GitHub
may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and referring
URL as part of their standard hosting infrastructure.

This data collection is governed exclusively by GitHub's own policies:

- :fontawesome-brands-github: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

---

## Embedded Music Players: Bandcamp

On select **pinned release-list pages**, this blog embeds mini music players from
[Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com), a service operated by **Songtradr, Inc.**
When your browser loads a page containing a Bandcamp player, it establishes a
direct connection to Bandcamp's servers to retrieve the player content.
As a result, Bandcamp may:

- receive your IP address and browser information,
- set cookies on your device,
- track interactions with the embedded player.

This data collection is governed entirely by Bandcamp's own policies:

- :material-music: [Bandcamp Privacy Policy](https://bandcamp.com/privacy)

!!! info "Lazy loading"
    Bandcamp players are loaded lazily — the player only contacts Bandcamp's
    servers once it is scrolled into your viewport, not on initial page load.

---

## Author Avatar

The author avatar displayed on post pages is loaded from
[GitHub's avatar service](https://avatars.githubusercontent.com). This causes your
browser to make a request to GitHub's servers and is subject to
[GitHub's General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

---

## Search

The blog's search functionality is entirely **client-side**. The search index is
downloaded once to your browser, and all queries are processed locally — no
search terms or results are transmitted to any external server.

The *share search* feature generates a shareable URL containing your query as a
URL fragment (`#`). This fragment is never sent to the server as part of an HTTP
request.

---

## RSS Feed

This blog provides an [RSS feed](https://soundphilosopher.github.io/music-chamber-blog/feed_rss_created.xml)
so you can follow new posts with an RSS reader. Subscribing is a purely passive
action: your reader periodically fetches the feed file from GitHub Pages. No
personal data is collected by this blog in that process.

## Your Rights

Since this blog does not collect any personal data itself, there is nothing held
about you here to access, correct, or delete.

If you are concerned about data processed by the third parties described above
(GitHub / Bandcamp / Songtradr), please refer directly to their respective privacy
policies linked above and exercise your rights through their official channels.

If you are located in the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a
complaint with your national supervisory authority (data protection authority).
